## The fortune

> Soon your newest leaf will unfurl toward the sun, and your keeper will tell everyone it was all their doing.

One kind, funny fortune for the houseplant on the windowsill — a prediction about the plant's own future, warm toward both the plant and its keeper.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the fortune (a single line, 108 characters) to `/output/entry.txt`.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
